CrewGuard Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

CrewGuard is a private app that lets seafarers keep a dated record of incidents, daily logs and supporting evidence. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your records stay on your phone. We do not collect them, we do not have servers that receive them, and we cannot see them.

Information stored on your device

Everything you enter is saved only in the app's private storage on your phone: your name and position, a PIN you choose, company and ship names, incident narratives, daily logs, witness details, reminders, and the photos, videos, audio recordings, documents and links you attach as proof. This information is not sent to the developer or to any third party.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Camera and microphone: to take photos, record video and record audio as evidence.
  • Photos, videos and files: to attach media or documents you choose from your phone.
  • Location: only if you choose to add your position to an entry. It is saved on your phone with that entry.
  • Notifications: to show reminders that you set. They are scheduled on your phone.
  • Biometrics / device lock: where offered, to unlock the app. The app never receives your fingerprint or face data.

You can change these permissions at any time in your phone's settings.

Sharing and exports

CrewGuard only sends information off your phone when you choose to. You can export a case as a PDF or ZIP, create a backup file, or share a file with another app (for example email, a messaging app or cloud storage). When you do, the file goes to the app and person you select. From that point it is handled by that service under its own policy. Exported files and backups are not encrypted, so share them only with people you trust, such as your lawyer.

If you attach a link (for example a video link), the app only stores the address and opens it in your browser or another app when you tap it.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-up and no servers that receive your records.
  • No advertising, no ad identifiers and no analytics or tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.

Keeping your data safe

Data lives in the app's private storage and each profile is protected by a PIN. Because the data exists only on your device, losing or resetting your phone, or uninstalling the app, can permanently delete your records unless you made a backup. Please back up regularly and keep backup files private.

Deleting your data

You can delete records inside the app, or remove everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in your phone's settings. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

CrewGuard is intended for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
